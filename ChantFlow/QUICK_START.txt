📱 MOBILE INSTALLATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

iPhone/iPad:
  1. Open in Safari
  2. Tap Share button
  3. Select "Add to Home Screen"
  4. App installs like native app!

Android:
  1. Open in Chrome
  2. Tap menu (⋮)
  3. Select "Install app"
  4. App installs on home screen!
